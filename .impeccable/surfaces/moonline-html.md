Experience · Moonline is an illustrated rhythm journey for children and families, with a subordinate touch composition studio. Its job is to make hearing a score, following its cues, and becoming one of its parts feel like one experience. An open night landscape takes the viewport; a copper departure ticket and an instrument integrated into the cab frame explain action without a dashboard. The signature moment is a note crossing the horizon and striking its own key just as the accompaniment reaches its beat. Prioritize touch reach, legibility and audio-clock synchronization in both languages; avoid requiring fullscreen browser APIs or MIDI hardware.
