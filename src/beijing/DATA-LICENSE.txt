BEIJING NETWORK DATABASE

The original illustrative map positions in network-data.json are derived from
OpenStreetMap geographic station coordinates with independently curated operating
facts and map-only interpolation for unmatched stations.

The adapted machine-readable database is supplied in network-data.json and at:
https://github.com/micwu-personal/TransitTales/blob/main/src/beijing/network-data.json
The ODbL attribution and adapted-database obligations apply to this database,
not to the independently authored game code, original scene art or music.

Operating facts were independently curated from BJMTR official line rosters,
station distance tables and Beijing government operating notices. Website map
images, provider schematic layouts, branded art and Amap database contents are
not included. Source URLs, dated snapshot, scope and simulated-attribute limits
are recorded in network-data.json.provenance.
